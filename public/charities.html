Our story
Why We Built This

The best charities in Singapore
are drowning in spreadsheets.

Every year, thousands of dedicated volunteers and charity staff spend their nights reconciling donation records, chasing down missing NRICs, and manually preparing IRAS submissions — instead of doing the work they actually care about.

The big charities have entire teams for this. The smaller ones don't. And yet the smaller ones are often doing the most important, most personal, most life-changing work in our communities.

We felt that wasn't right. So we built something to fix it.

Sound familiar?
